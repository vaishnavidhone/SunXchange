
# ☀️ SunXchange
### Power your community. Share the surplus. Shape a sustainable future.

**SunXchange** is an AI-powered community solar energy-sharing platform that explores how surplus rooftop solar energy can be better utilized within residential communities.

By combining intelligent energy forecasting, surplus-to-demand matching, and community-level energy management, SunXchange aims to make renewable energy more accessible, efficient, and affordable.

> Your rooftop could power more than just your home.

---

## 🌍 The Problem

- ☀️ **Unequal access:** Not every household can install rooftop solar panels.
- ⚡ **Underutilized energy:** Solar generation and household demand do not always align.
- 🏢 **Disconnected communities:** Residents lack coordinated ways to manage local energy needs.
- 💸 **Energy costs:** Households need smarter ways to understand and manage electricity consumption.

## 💡 Our Solution

SunXchange envisions a connected energy ecosystem where solar producers and consumers can participate in community-based energy coordination.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Peer-to-Peer Energy Sharing** — Explore surplus energy sharing between participating households.
- **Apartment Energy Management** — Bring community-level energy coordination into one platform.
- **AI-Powered Forecasting** — Estimate solar generation and electricity demand.
- **Intelligent Energy Matching** — Match available surplus with consumer requirements.
- **Energy Analytics Dashboard** — Track generation, consumption, allocation, and estimated savings.
- **Electricity Bill History** — Review historical bills and understand consumption trends.
- **Sustainability Insights** — Explore renewable energy utilization and potential environmental benefits.

> Feature availability depends on the current implementation.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Connect:** Add relevant solar generation and electricity consumption information.
2. **Forecast:** Estimate upcoming generation and demand.
3. **Match:** Identify potential matches between surplus energy and consumer requirements.
4. **Coordinate:** Present proposed energy allocations and insights.
5. **Track:** Review energy usage, historical bills, and estimated savings.
